Privacy Policy

2026.11.13

PaleteWorks ("we") respects the privacy of everyone who uses Travler (the "App"). 1. Information we collect The App has no sign-up or login. We do not collect or store personal information such as your name, phone number, or email on any server. 2. Information stored only on your device Your plans (titles, dates, schedules, links, memos, colors), photos attached to schedules, and app settings (language, date format, dark mode) are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. They are removed when you delete the App. If automatic device backup (Google backup on Android, iCloud backup on iOS) is on, this data may be included in backups to your own account and restored when you reinstall the App or move to a new device. We cannot access these backups. 3. Photo access The App accesses only the photos you choose, to attach them to a schedule. Chosen photos are copied into the App and never uploaded. If you don't allow access, every feature except attaching photos keeps working. 4. Ads and automatically collected information The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve ads, Google may collect your advertising ID, IP address, device information (model, operating system), and ad impressions and clicks, and use them to deliver and personalize ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. This information is collected and processed by Google; we do not receive it in a form that identifies you. · Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses information from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites You can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads). If you are in a region where consent is required, such as the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent to personalized ads and related purposes on first launch, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > General > Ad privacy settings. You can use every feature of the App even if you do not consent. If you live in a U.S. state with a consumer privacy law, such as California, Colorado, or Virginia, providing the information above to Google for personalized ads may count as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under that law. The App shows you a notice about this, and you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information at any time in Settings > General > Ad privacy settings. If you opt out, you will see only non-personalized ads, and every feature of the App keeps working. 5. Version check To tell you about updates, the App looks up its latest version on the app store. No information that identifies you is sent. 6. Retention and deletion We do not keep personal information. Data on your device is removed when you delete it in the App or delete the App. Copies in device backups are kept or deleted according to your backup settings. Information collected by Google is kept and deleted under Google's policies. 7. Sharing and processors We do not share your personal information with third parties or outsource its processing. The ad provider (Google) collects the information in section 4 directly, and it may be transferred outside your country (for example, to the United States). 8. Your rights You can edit or delete your data in the App at any time, and change photo access and ad personalization in your device settings. For anything else, contact us below. If you live in a U.S. state with a consumer privacy law, you have the right to access, correct, and delete your personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing and of targeted advertising. Because we do not keep your personal information, you can edit or delete data in the App yourself, and you can opt out of sale or sharing in Ad privacy settings (section 4). Requests about information collected by Google follow Google's Privacy Policy, and we will help you if you contact us. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. 9. Children The App is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. 10. Contact · Operator: PaleteWorks · Email: paleteworks@gmail.com 11.
